Your hypothesis holds at these assumptions: two reps is worse than one rep plus you. The second rep converts your time into cash at a worse rate than you do. That makes the second hire a lifestyle decision, not a revenue one — which is a perfectly good reason to make it, but worth knowing that's what you're buying.
