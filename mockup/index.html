02 Is the second rep worth it? the question you actually asked
You only · 5 audits/wk

cash/mo. No commission. Highest close rate, but your calendar is full and delivery is starved.
1 rep + you at 2.5/wk
$341k
cash/mo after commission. You keep the best-fit calls, rep takes volume. Best of the three.
2 reps, you at 0
$327k
cash/mo after commission. More volume, but at a lower close rate and double commission — and it buys back your time rather than more money.
Your hypothesis holds at these assumptions: two reps is worse than one rep plus you. The second rep converts your time into cash at a worse rate than you do. That makes the second hire a lifestyle decision, not a revenue one — which is a perfectly good reason to make it, but worth knowing that's what you're buying.
