05  How the throttle behaves

  • It eases off, it does not slam. Audits run at full pace until the queue reaches half the threshold, then taper to zero at the threshold. A hard on/off switch makes the model oscillate — sell hard, stop dead, sell hard — which is not how a calendar behaves.
  • The threshold is measured in months of delivery, not headcount. A queue of 3 means nothing on its own; 3 clients against a capacity of 1.3 starts a month is over two months of backlog. That is what the lever counts.
  • A delivery hire re-opens selling automatically. More capacity means the same queue is fewer months deep, so the throttle lifts on its own. No extra lever.
  • The paused tag, status line and red audit cell already exist in the model’s code — they are styled and never fire. Wiring this switches them on.
